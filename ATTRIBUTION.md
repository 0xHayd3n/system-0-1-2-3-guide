## Research credit

The research is by **Tadahiro Taniguchi, Yasushi Hirai, Masahiro Suzuki, Shingo Murata, Takato Horii, and Kazutoshi Tanaka**, in that order. Source: _System 0/1/2/3: Quad-process theory for multi-timescale embodied collective cognitive systems_, [arXiv:2503.06138v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06138v3), dated 16 July 2025.

The paper's title page lists Kyoto University and Ritsumeikan University (Taniguchi), Keio University (Hirai and Murata, with separate listed addresses), the University of Tokyo (Suzuki and Horii), Osaka University (Horii), and OMRON SINIC X Corporation (Tanaka). These are the source's affiliations, not current affiliation checks. Taniguchi is listed as corresponding author. [Title page](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.06138v3#page=1)

The authors' synthesis builds on many cited researchers and traditions. In particular, §1 attributes CPC to Taniguchi (2024), while §3.2 Figure 4 is explicitly reused from that work. Passive dynamics, neurorobotics, emergent communication, and Bergsonian ideas predate this guide and, in many cases, the paper. The reading modules distinguish that reviewed work from the proposed synthesis. Consult the paper's full references at [PDF pp.31–41](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.06138v3#page=31).
